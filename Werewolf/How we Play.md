## Time
- When I wake up: I check everybody's night time activities and narrate the consequences. This starts the day phase.
- I will make a poll of the surviving people. During the day phase, you may vote and unvote as you please.
- When I go to bed: The night phase starts. You can send me your targets and such.

## Roles:
*This will be updated as we get deeper into it.*
- 2 x Werewolves: You win if you kill the villagers. If ever there are an equal amount of werewolves and villagers, the wolves win.
- 1 x Fortune Teller: At night, you pick a target to see if they are villager or werewolf.
- The rest: Villagers: You win if you vote out all the werewolves.

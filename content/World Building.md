## Big Picture
- **World:** Our modern Earth as a model, this world has evolved in parallel. It shares languages, cultures, technology etc. But not history, people, events. I am writing this from a 2010ish perspective.
- **Starting Location:** In the small city of Amberport, nestled in the Oak District lies Little Wolf Street. There you will find the Bee and Bunny Pub, a place for magical and non-magical folk to enjoy evening cocktails, meals and stories.
- **Present World Events:** *see [[Campaign Handout]]*
- **Magic:** Magic is old in this world, and for the safety and sanity of normals, it has been kept secret. The All-King and his council have been working together to keep the existence of magic on the down low.

## Story and Plot
- *What is the campaign about?*
	- At the time of writing this, the main idea is the party starts working for a patron at the Bee and Bunny Pub. The players will make their characters and Arcs and give reasons why they work here.
	- Running and managing the Bee and Bunny is not the main drive of the game. They will meet NPCs who have "real-world" problems, business folk who want to do deals, shady types who wish harm on the business.
- *What tropes am I aiming for?*
	- **Magic is secret, do not let the normals find out.**
	- Alternate history, the world is different than our Earth, but feels similar.
	- Supernatural Hotspot, this place is a bit more magic than others.

## Elder Notes
- *Sources of inspiration for this campaign setting:*
	- He who fights monsters is classical fantasy that touches modern magic.
	- Dungeon Crawler Carl blends fantasy and magic with video game.
	- Heretical Fishing is a cozy RPG where combat is very optional and the world has stuff to do.
	- Legends and Lattes is a cozy RPG where the main character just wants to own a shop.
	- Dresden Files is a good example of how magic fits into a living world.
	- Supernatural is a good example of folk lore and mythic as a quest engine.
- *How do my [[Icons]] fit into this Modern Magic setting?*
	- **The All-King** - The head of a board that balances the interests of various magical factions.
	- **The Enlightened One** - A mystic who is not from any time that ensures all timelines are kept.
	- **The Knight** - Champion for the physical world ensures that human-kind does not get over run by powerful, magical forces.
	- **The One who Speaks** - Seeks to preserve the history of this world and to ensure honesty in all things.
	- **The Priest** - Still a speaker of the old gods who ensures their wills are kept and their voices heard.
	- **The Unnamed** - Disguised as a non-profit who seeks to help heal the world and its people.
	- **The Shepherd** - Established organizations that help guide people who lost their way. Psychologists, clergy, 911 operators and the like try and operate for the Shepherd.
	- **The Spearman** - A specialized task force who has since ancient times guarded the world from a long forgotten evil. (It could be Chtulu, Abyssal creatures, hell itself. Depends on us.)
	- **The Atrophied** - In charge of cults who summon dark gods.
	- **The Courtesan** - Network of spies who aim to trade in secrets.
	- **The Outcasted Fey** - A collector of the old magics and artifacts.
	- **The Berserker**- Non-magical gangs who seek to out and destroy magic.
	- **The King of Crossroads** - Banks, lawyers and investors connected to help the rich get richer.
	- **The Blade in the Dark** - A shadow figure who oversees the dark, grimy underbelly of most cities.
	- **The Conqueror** - A dictator who preaches peace while bombing nations.
	- **The Summoner** - A world-hopping opportunist that turns souls into magic.

This is just a quick and dirty "how currency works" thing.


| Cypher            | World                | Explained                                              |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Inexpensive       | Copper               | Common, everyday people buy this on the regular.       |
| Moderately priced | Silver               | Common people buy this, sometimes and only one or two. |
| Expensive         | Gold                 | This strains a commoner's budget.                      |
| Very Expensive    | Platinum             | Luxury items that people save up for.                  |
| Exorbitant        | Mithral              | Only the rich buy these.                               |
| Priceless         | You can't afford it. |                                                        |
## How it works:
- On your character sheet, if ever you have 15+ of a currency, remove 10 and add 1 to the next tier.
	- 15 silver -> 1 gold and 5 silver.
- If you want to go from gold to silver, there will be some wastage. Roll a d6+ Intellect Edge.
	- 1 silver -> 1d6 + IE copper.
- When you buy something, subtract 1 from your currency pool. BUT if you have 10 or less:
	- Roll 1d10 and if you exceed your current resource pool, then reduce resource by 1.
	- Else, you keep your current pool.

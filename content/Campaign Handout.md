## Six Truths
*The six truths are things that are happening in the world right now. They are meant to advertise possible conflicts and they set the stage for the campaign. We might touch on some or all of these. But you can think of them as happening in the background, and if you did nothing, something will still come from it. **This is OUT OF CHARACTER knowledge***

1. Servants of the Priest have had their hands full lately as street violence is on the rise.
2. A gang belonging to the Berserker has bene terrorizing Amberport. They seem to be going after magical creatures.
3. A new non-profit organization has started advocating for the protection of parks in the city. They are having trouble getting local traction.
4. A for-profit mogul has been buying parks in order to turn them into parking garages and fuel stations.
5. The One who Speaks has been under investigation for revealing magic that they swear they never revealed.
6. The Courtesan's newest branch have successfully infiltrated many, many homes and secrets are no longer safe.

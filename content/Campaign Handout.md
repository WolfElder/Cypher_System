## Six Truths
1. Agents of the **Courtesan** are turning a faction of the **One who Speaks** against the public in order to out a secret.
2. The **One who Speaks** does not keep many secrets, but those that are kept must be protected. 
3. Followers of the **Berserker** have started a genocide against nomadic monks. The monks have given vows of pacifism.
4. An order of the **Priest** is looking for recruits to push back against the **Berserker**'s forces.
5. The **Outcasted Fey** has been ripping fonts of magic from natural sources, leaving rot and decay in their wake.
6. The **Unnamed** has received the plight of druids, and has sent word in the wind to call for anyone willing to help to come to their aid.

## The Core Idea:
A common relative has passed away and left his farm to the party. For whatever reason, you are now responsible for rehabilitating the rundown farm and turning it into your dream home. To aid you, you have a butler of sorts: George the Squirrel. He will keep you up to date on the farm's needs.

## Connections to the farmer:
1. Family -> grandkids, nieces and nephews, etc.
2. Close Friends -> friends of the farmer or the friend's kids.
3. Worker -> you worked the farm before the farmer passed and want to stay on.
4. Happenstance -> You arrived looking for work the same day the party arrived.
5. Homeless Druid -> Due to world events, you find yourself without grove and the plight of the farm seems like a worthwhile cause.
6. Good friend -> You are accompanying your friend on their new adventure.

## [[The Farm]]
The farm will have the following pools:
- Health -> This is how healthy, repaired, cared for your farm is.
- Yield -> This is the amount of money/goods you can expect from your farm per month.
- Mood -> How cozy, magical and not-cursed your farm feels.
- Slots -> This is how many empty space the farm has for new things. Each time you want a new operation, field, shed it will take up a space. You get more by upgrading the farm.
- Upkeep -> The cost per month for owning the farm. It starts at zero, but then becomes more expensive as you add things.

As of writing this, George has three quests for you already.
1. The Dried Up Well: Health
	1. The well on the farm that provides water to all the homes and crops has dried up. It's source is in a nearby forest and needs to be repaired.
	2. Reward: Health +1, Water.
2. Your First Structure: Yield, Slot
	1. Every farm needs a thing that brings in some money. Be it a coup, a corral, or a plot to farm grain on. Obtain the materials to make this building. (George recommends a coup)
	2. Reward Yield +1, Building.
3. Something Feels Off: Mood.
	1. At night the farm does not rest well. Something keeps you from sleeping. It bares further investigation.
	2. Reward: Mood +1, Exhaustion -1.

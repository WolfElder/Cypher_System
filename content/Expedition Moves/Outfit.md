When you **prepare for an expedition in a friendly community**, mark as many spaces on your Inventory insert as you wish to carry, either on specific items or in “undefined.” Mark up to 3 spaces for a light load (quick and quiet), 4-6 spaces for a normal load, or 7-9 spaces for a heavy load (noisy, slow, quick to tire). Also, mark a number of spaces in the **Small Items** section equal to 4+Prosperity (again, on specific items or in Undefined).

You can select:
- Items printed on the Inventory sheet 
- Other common, mundane items
- Any of your special possessions 
- Special items for which you Trade & Barter

Tell the GM what you’re bringing and answer their questions about your gear and where you got it.

## Notes
- Small items = 4 + Prosperity
- Undefined = As many as you like, use [[Have What You Need]]

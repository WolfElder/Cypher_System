*These are the movers and shakers of the world. They are not gods, they are not people you should meet. But they are concepts/ideas that cause people in the world to do things.*

### Good(ish)
1. **The All-King** - A just Speaker who keeps power in balance.
2. **The Enlightened One** - A spiritual Adept who gazes outward and through time.
3. **The Knight** - A valiant Warrior who has sworn an oath to protect the physical world.
4. **The One who Speaks** - An honest Adept who seeks the truth in all things.
5. **The Priest** - A compassionate Speaker who speaks with gods of the light.
6. **The Unnamed** - A sacrificed Explorer who seeks to absolve pain.
7. **The Shepherd** - A humble Explorer who finds what is lost.
8. **The Spearman** - A honorable Warrior who holds the darkness at bay.

### Evil(ish)
1. **The Atrophied** - A hateful Adept who communes with dark gods.
2. **The Courtesan** - A deceitful Speaker who corrupts purposes and infiltrates hearts.
3. **The Outcasted Fey** - A cowardly Adept who steals magic.
4. **The Berserker**- A tyrannical Warrior who wants to eliminate the weak.
5. **The King of Crossroads**- A selfish Explorer who makes unsavory deals.
6. **The Blade in the Dark**- A dishonorable Explorer who consorts with criminals. 
7. **The Conqueror** - A materialistic Warrior who seeks to take all lands.
8. **The Summoner** - A prideful Speaker who reaches across planes and pulls souls.
